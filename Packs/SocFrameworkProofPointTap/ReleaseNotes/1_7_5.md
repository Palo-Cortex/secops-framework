
#### Correlation Rules

##### SOC Proofpoint TAP - Threat Detected All Alerts

- **fw_url_domain** and **dns_query_name** were null on every alert whose threat URL sat on a non-public TLD. `extract_url_registered_domain` resolves against the public suffix list, so any internal or non-routable domain (`.local`, `.internal`, `.corp`) returned null. Both fields now derive from `extract_url_host`.
- Emitting the host rather than the registered domain is also the correct grouping value independent of that bug. `dns_query_name` is matched by exact equality and CrowdStrike emits the name as queried (`dns_requests[].domain_name`), so a registered domain here would mean TAP reporting `evil.com` while the endpoint reports `cdn.evil.com` — the two would never pivot.
