## [1.7.2] - September 2026

### Changed

#### SOC Proofpoint TAP - Threat Detected All Alerts — reverted to the 1.5.6 content

The rule, both layouts and the modeling rule are restored to the state shipped in
1.5.6, the last version confirmed working on a tenant. The vendor contract at
`schemas/vendors/proofpoint-tap/` is reverted with them, so the next emit
regenerates this content rather than reintroducing the newer version.

What 1.5.6 restores, and why each mattered:

**Suppression works again.** 1.5.6 suppresses on `GUID` + `type`, both real
top-level dataset columns at that revision. The 1.7.x line kept the same two
field names after moving every column read into `rawJSON` — but suppression
resolves against the dataset, not against the detection query's `alter` output,
so it has been keyed on a field that does not exist. Suppression was never
disabled; it was silently non-resolving, which presents as duplicate issues.

**The two Proofpoint layouts return.** `Proofpoint - Click Permitted` and
`Proofpoint - Message Delivered` were removed in 1.6.0 on the grounds that
`group: incident` layouts do not belong in a marketplacev2 pack. That argument
is about packaging, not about analyst value, and removing them left Proofpoint
issues without their layout.

**The modeling rule returns.** Dropped in 1.7.1 to clear a pack-install failure;
1.5.6's version installed cleanly.

Scheduling is unchanged and already matches framework policy: `SCHEDULED`,
`*/10 * * * *`, 20-minute search window. A DC who wants real-time edits one
field and the scheduled settings survive underneath.

### Known risk

1.6.0 rewrote 24 column reads to come from `rawJSON` because the pack could not
install on a tenant that had not yet ingested parsed Proofpoint events —
referencing an absent column fails XQL validation at install and surfaces as a
bare `101704`. Reverting to 1.5.6 reinstates flat column reads and therefore
reinstates that failure mode for tenants with no parsed TAP data.

This is a deliberate trade: 1.5.6 is known-good on tenants that have TAP data,
and the 1.7.x line is broken on them. Verify install on a tenant without parsed
Proofpoint events before treating this as safe everywhere.
