#### Scripts

##### SOCFWConfig

- New `command=owner`: give a framework JOB an owner who is a user on the tenant.
  `!SOCFWConfig command=owner job="Auto Triage V3" owner=<user>`. Checked against the
  tenant's users.
- New `command=job`: turn a framework JOB's schedule on or off.
  `!SOCFWConfig command=job job="Auto Triage V3" value=on`. Refused while the owner is not
  a user, because the platform would accept it and every run would error.
- Both are dry runs unless `apply=yes`, read back after writing, and record a
  `config_change` row.
- `status` gains a Framework JOBs section: schedule, owner, whether the owner is a user,
  next run.
- `actor` and `job` are dropdowns. `actor` offers analyst, ai and automation.

##### SOCCommandWrapper

- The `Action_Actor` dropdown offers what callers pass: analyst, ai, automation, layout
  (default analyst). It no longer offers `shadow` or `system`, which simulated every
  action that could change something.
- `issue_emergency` and `case_lifecycle` are no longer accepted as actors; nothing
  passed them.

#### Lists

##### SOCFrameworkActions_V3

- `soc-star-case` `shadow_mode_by_actor` names analyst, ai and automation
  (`issue_emergency` removed). No change in what runs.

##### SOCOptimizationConfig_V3

- Removed `Triage Incidents JOB.enabled`. Nothing read it; the JOB's own schedule is
  the on/off.

#### Jobs

- Auto Triage V3 ships owned by the placeholder `set-owner-with-SOCFWConfig` instead of
  a named person. It still installs off; use `SOCFWConfig command=job` to start it.
