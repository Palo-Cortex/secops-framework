#### Scripts

##### SOCFWElevateCase

- Every `elevate_decision` row now carries the assessment's `story` (WHAT FIRED, EXPOSURE, VERDICT,
  NEXT CHECK, joined into one string) and `closure_blockers`, so a list of what would be starred
  shows why without opening each issue. Rows written without an assessment are unchanged.

##### SOCFWEmergencyResponse

- Every `emergency_decision` row now carries the assessment's `story` and `closure_blockers` in the
  same form, so a containment or escalation decision can be reviewed from the dataset alone.
