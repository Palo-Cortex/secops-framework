#### Scripts

##### SOCFWPhaseExecutor

- A step handed to `SOCCommandWrapper` now reports what the wrapper said happened: `executed`,
  `shadow`, `skipped` or `failed`. It used to report `dispatched` for every step sent, including
  steps that only ran in shadow and steps whose vendor command failed.

##### SOCFWCaseContainment

- `containment_state` is `contained` only when every step really ran. A plan that ran entirely in
  shadow is `simulated`; a failed or skipped step counts against containment like a capability gap.
- A planner or executor that fails now reads `error`, not `nothing_to_contain`.
- The `case_containment` row adds `steps_executed`, `steps_simulated` and `steps_failed`.
  `steps_dispatched` still counts every step handed to the wrapper.
