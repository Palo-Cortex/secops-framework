#### Scripts

##### SOCFWPhaseExecutor

- A step handed to `SOCCommandWrapper` now reports what the wrapper said happened: `executed`,
  `shadow`, `skipped` or `failed`. It used to report `dispatched` for every step sent, including
  steps that only ran in shadow and steps whose vendor command failed.

##### SOCFWConfig

- `command=job` can turn the `SOC Case Response` JOB on and off and set its owner.

##### SOCFWCaseContainment

- Removed. Case-level response runs through `SOCFWCaseResponse` in the NIST IR AI pack, which runs
  each step inside an issue of the case that carries the step's entity. A tenant that has the script
  keeps it until the pack is uninstalled; nothing in the framework calls it.
