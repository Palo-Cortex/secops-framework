#### Scripts

##### SOCFWConfig

- New `command=mode`. With no arguments it lists the modes in `SOCFWConfigModes` next to this
  tenant's current settings and says which mode the tenant matches (or `custom`).
- `mode=<name>` applies one: it sets every shadow switch the mode names, for the automated actors
  only, and turns the Auto Triage JOB on. `owner=<email>` sets the JOB's owner; without it, a JOB
  that has no owner stays off and the rest is applied.
- `mode=default` removes the mode settings and turns Auto Triage off, back to what the pack
  installs.
- A mode never changes the Auto Triage threshold or window, analyst actions, or the emergency
  protect and role settings. Dry run unless `apply=yes`. Every change is recorded as a
  `config_change` row.
- `status` now shows the Auto Triage score threshold and age window (`SOCFWConfig.Status.triage`)
  and which mode the tenant matches (`SOCFWConfig.Status.mode`).

#### Lists

##### SOCFWConfigModes

- New. Four modes, each setting the same switches so changing modes leaves nothing behind:
  - `pov`: starring and benign AI close run for real, Auto Triage on, all containment recorded in
    shadow.
  - `star_and_triage`: starring runs for real, Auto Triage on; the AI closes nothing and nothing is
    contained.
  - `contain_identity_and_hosts`: `pov` plus severity raise, closing contained issues, and
    emergency revoke of sessions and tokens and isolation of workstations.
  - `contain_all`: every automated action in the list runs for real, including emergency email
    retract and domain or address blocks.
