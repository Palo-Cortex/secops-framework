# 29 Oct 2026 — Emergency Response blocks C2 destinations

**What you get:** an endpoint issue on a Command and Control detection, malicious at
high confidence, blocks the address and domain it talked to, without waiting for the
case. Only destinations a reputation source scores malicious are blocked; internal,
unscored and protected ones are escalated for a person. In a PoV the blocks are
shadowed, so the run records what would have been blocked.

**Changed:** `soc-optimization-unified` pack (`endpoint_c2` policy,
`SOCFWEmergencyResponse`, `soc-block-network-indicator`, `!SOCFWConfig command=emergency`
`tactic=` and `domain:`) · `soc-framework-nist-ir-ai` pack (`SOCFrameworkNormalizeMap_NIST_IR`:
endpoint `remoteip` → `SOCFramework.Endpoint.remote_ips`).

**Update:**
1. Pack → `soc-optimization-unified` 3.29.0 or later
2. Pack → `soc-framework-nist-ir-ai` 1.6.0 or later
3. Keep your own domains from ever being blocked:
   `!SOCFWConfig command=emergency protect=domain:<your domain> apply=yes`
4. See the policies: `!SOCFWConfig command=emergency`
